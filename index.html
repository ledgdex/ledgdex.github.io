LEDGDEX DEX

Docs · Viewer · Live Markets · About

Viewer (Verify any ledgdex ledger in your browser. Keep your own ledger, sign messages to sellers and collect their receipts. Your key stays in this browser, sealed with your passphrase.)

Docs (Getting started, markets, selling and buying, the command line, the API and tested examples.)


About (Ledgdex is a simple market on a dex. Each self keeps a signed ledger that only grows, in its own dex.)

Live Markets (These ledgdexes trade in public. Each one is an ordinary dex with its ledger in it. Read its pages, or load its ledger in the viewer and verify each signature and hash yourself.)
