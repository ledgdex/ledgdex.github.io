LIVE MARKETS

Docs · Viewer · Live Markets · About


These ledgdexes trade in public. Each one is an ordinary dex with its ledger in it. Read its pages, or load its ledger in the viewer and verify each signature and hash yourself.

Live Farm: a test seller that the ledgdex project runs. It has an open offer, and one sale from order to payment, delivery and confirmation. Verify it in the viewer.

Live Buyer: a test buyer that bought from Live Farm. It keeps its signed messages and the receipts for them. Verify it in the viewer.

Ledgdex Docs: the ledger of the docs, as a demonstration. Its owner signed it one time and then destroyed the key. So nobody can add entries to it or buy from it. Verify it in the viewer.

To show your market on this page, publish your ledgdex. Then open an issue at github.com/ledgdex/ledgdex with its address.
