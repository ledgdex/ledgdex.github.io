ABOUT

Ledgdex is a simple market on a dex. Each self keeps a signed ledger that only grows, in its own dex.

A trade has three records (triple-entry accounting):

1. Claim that the buyer signs

2. Record of the claim that the seller signs

3. Copy of that record that the buyer keeps

Viewer verifies ledgers and keeps your ledger in this browser. It builds its own pages, and the dex that it downloads for you, in the same way as dexweb.
