Ledgdex is a simple market on a dex. Each self keeps a signed ledger that only grows, in its own dex.

A trade has three records:

- the claim that the buyer signs

- the record of the claim that the seller signs

- the copy of that record that the buyer keeps

The viewer verifies ledgers and keeps your ledger in this browser. It builds its own pages, and the dex that it downloads for you, in the same way as dexweb.
