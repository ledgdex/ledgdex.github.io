USE CASES


A ledgdex is for trades between people or computers that keep their own records. They want these records to be evidence.

a seller that sells to regular buyers   offers, claims, payment notices, receipts
an exchange of sellers                 a root (admission), an index (discovery) and sellers
freelance services                     an offer for each service, delivered with a link, disputes to a guild
digital goods and licences             a seller bot that delivers after payment
early access, one-of-a-kind items     sealed-bid auctions, with a reserve
procurement and tenders                "best": "lowest" auctions: the lowest bid under the budget wins
robots and machines that sell service  device keys, a claim for each unit, settlement each day
a club or co-operative                 admission through a root, offers for members only
public records of supply               notes and offers as dated, signed statements

A ledgdex is not for these jobs:

- Trades that need one shared global order, or instant finality between strangers. Ledgdex has no consensus, by design.

- Money itself. Payment occurs outside the ledger, and the ledger records it.
