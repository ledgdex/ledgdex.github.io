SET UP A MARKETPLACE


A market can serve a place, a trade or a community. It has no platform in the middle. Each member keeps their own ledgdex.

One operator keeps a ledger that has two jobs:

- The index: the list of shops in the market.

- The root: the list of members that the operator admits.

Shops sell to admitted members. Buyers find offers through the index. The operator is the default arbiter of disputes. Example: Marketplace does all of these steps on one computer.

1. The operator makes the ledgdex of the market and publishes it (Publishing a Ledgdex Online): 

ledgdex init market --name "Ratnagiri Market" --about "Fruit sellers of Ratnagiri" --key market \
    --url https://market.example --dest git@github.com:YOU/market.git --branch main
ledgdex publish market

2. Each shop makes its own ledgdex in the market: 

ledgdex init farm --name "Mango Farm" --key farm --root https://market.example

3. Shops and buyers send their public key to the operator. The commands ledgdex keygen NAME and ledgdex init show the public key.

4. The operator checks who they are, outside the ledger. Then the operator admits them: 

ledgdex admit market ed25519:SHOP_KEY --name "Mango Farm"
ledgdex admit market ed25519:BUYER_KEY --name "Asha"
ledgdex publish market

5. The operator lists the shops: 

ledgdex list market https://farm.example --note "mangoes"

6. A shop can offer to admitted members only, with "allow": "admitted". The shop then also admits the buyers that it serves, with ledgdex admit farm KEY. A shop can also offer to anyone, with "any".

7. Buyers read the index to find offers: 

ledgdex discover https://market.example --offers

8. Each day, each member runs ledgdex check on the ledgers that they trade with (Checking for Tampering). The check finds each rewrite, with signed proof.

The operator decides who is a member and decides disputes. The operator signs these decisions, and anyone can read them.

The operator cannot change the ledger of a member, hide a sale or set a price. The operator's key is the most valuable key in the market.

Keep the operator key offline, and use a device key each day (Keys, Devices and Recovery).

Other ways to run a market:

- Without --root, a market is only an index. Anyone can be in its list.

- Many markets can list the same shop.

- A shop with no root is the arbiter of its own offers, unless it names an arbiter (Disputes and Arbiters).
